Explore The Beauty Of Uttar Pradesh

Uttar Pradesh is home to many historical monuments that represent India's rich history, architecture and culture. From the Taj Mahal in Agra to the Bara Imambara in Lucknow, the state has a fascinating heritage.

Popular Distination In Uttar Pradesh

1.Taj Mahal

  • Location: Agra
  • Famous for: Beauty
  • Made By: Akbar
  • Opening Time: 6:00Am
  • Closing Time: 6:00Pm
  • Days available: Full moon night, plus two days before and two days after (5 nights total per month, excluding Fridays and Ramadan)
  • Timing: 8:30 PM to 12:30 AM

2.Agra Fort

  • Location: Agra
  • Famous for: Agra Fort is famous as a massive 16th-century UNESCO World Heritage Site that served as the main royal residence and seat of power for the Mughal Empire.
  • Made By: Akbar
  • Time: 6:00 AM to 6:00 PM (sunrise to sunset), with last entry usually permitted around 5:00 PM to 5:30 PM

3.Bara Imambara

  • Location: Lucknow
  • Famous for: its massive pillarless central hall, the confusing labyrinth known as Bhool Bhulaiyaa, and its origin as an 18th-century famine relief project
  • Made By: Nawab Asaf-ud-Daula
  • Time: Opening Time: 6:00 AM Closing Time: 5:00 PM

4.Jhansi Fort

  • Location: Jhansi
  • Famous for: the historic stronghold where Rani Lakshmibai heroically led the fight against British colonial rule during the Revolt of 1857
  • Made By: Raja Bir Singh
  • Time: open daily from 6:00 AM to 6:00 PM

5.Chunar Fort

  • Location: Mirzapur
  • Famous for: its commanding position on a rocky cliff of the Vindhya Range overlooking the Ganges River
  • Made By: Chunar Fort does not have a single creator, as it was built, expanded, and modified by multiple rulers over more than two millennia.
  • Time: 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM

6. Itimad-ud-Daulah's Tomb

  • Location: Agra
  • Famous for: Its delicate white marble architecture, colourful marble inlay work and beautiful geometric designs. It is often called the "Baby Taj".
  • Built By: Nur Jahan
  • Timing: 6:00 AM to 6:00 PM

7. Akbar's Tomb

  • Location: Sikandra, Agra
  • Famous for: Its grand Mughal architecture, large gateway, beautiful gardens and the tomb of Emperor Akbar.
  • Built By: Akbar; completed by Jahangir
  • Timing: Sunrise to Sunset

8. Fatehpur Sikri

  • Location: Agra District
  • Famous for: Its historic Mughal city, Buland Darwaza, Jama Masjid, royal palaces and beautiful examples of Mughal architecture.
  • Built By: Emperor Akbar
  • Timing: Sunrise to Sunset

9. Chota Imambara

  • Location: Lucknow
  • Famous for: Its elegant Awadhi architecture, decorated interiors, chandeliers, domes and beautiful surrounding structures.
  • Built By: Muhammad Ali Shah
  • Timing: 6:00 AM to 6:00 PM

10. Rumi Darwaza

  • Location: Lucknow
  • Famous for: Its magnificent 18th-century gateway architecture and its importance as one of the most recognizable landmarks of Lucknow.
  • Built By: Nawab Asaf-ud-Daula
  • Timing: Generally accessible during the day

11. Lucknow Residency

  • Location: Lucknow
  • Famous for: Its important role during the Siege of Lucknow in the Revolt of 1857 and its preserved historic buildings and ruins.
  • Built By: British East India Company
  • Timing: 10:00 AM to 5:00 PM

12. Dhamek Stupa

  • Location: Sarnath, Varanasi
  • Famous for: Its importance in Buddhist history and its traditional association with the place where Buddha delivered his first sermon.
  • Built By: Developed under ancient Buddhist patronage
  • Timing: Sunrise to Sunset

13. Ramnagar Fort

  • Location: Ramnagar, Varanasi
  • Famous for: Its riverside location beside the Ganges, royal museum, historic architecture and connection with the former Maharaja of Banaras.
  • Built By: Balwant Singh
  • Timing: 10:00 AM to 5:00 PM

14. Ram Mandir

  • Location: Ayodhya
  • Famous for: Its grand modern temple architecture and its religious significance associated with the Ram Janmabhoomi site.
  • Built By: Shri Ram Janmabhoomi Teerth Kshetra
  • Timing: Visitor timings vary according to the darshan schedule

15. Kashi Vishwanath Temple

  • Location: Varanasi
  • Famous for: Its major religious and cultural importance, its association with Lord Shiva and its location near the sacred Ganges.
  • Built By: Present temple associated with Ahilyabai Holkar
  • Timing: Varies according to the daily darshan schedule

16. Akbar's Tomb

  • Location: Sikandra, Agra
  • Famous for: Grand Mughal architecture, large gateway, gardens and the tomb of Emperor Akbar.
  • Built By: Akbar; completed by Jahangir
  • Timing: Sunrise to sunset

17. Mehtab Bagh

  • Location: Agra
  • Famous for: Mughal garden and its beautiful view of the Taj Mahal across the Yamuna River.
  • Established By: Mughal rulers
  • Timing: Sunrise to sunset

18. Chini ka Rauza

  • Location: Agra
  • Famous for: Glazed tile decoration and Persian-influenced Mughal architecture.
  • Built For: Afzal Khan Shirazi
  • Timing: Sunrise to sunset

19. Tomb of Mariam-uz-Zamani

  • Location: Sikandra, Agra
  • Famous for: Historic Mughal tomb and its connection with Mariam-uz-Zamani, mother of Emperor Jahangir.
  • Built By: Jahangir
  • Timing: Sunrise to sunset

20. Ram Bagh

  • Location: Agra
  • Famous for: One of the oldest surviving Mughal gardens in India.
  • Established By: Emperor Babur
  • Timing: Sunrise to sunset

21. Chattar Manzil

  • Location: Lucknow
  • Famous for: Its distinctive umbrella-shaped dome and Nawabi-era architecture.
  • Built By: Nawabs of Awadh
  • Timing: Timings may vary

22. Dilkusha Kothi

  • Location: Lucknow
  • Famous for: European-style architecture and its historical connection with the events of 1857.
  • Built By: Major Gore Ouseley
  • Timing: Daytime; timings may vary

23. Hussainabad Clock Tower

  • Location: Lucknow
  • Famous for: Its Victorian-Gothic clock tower and historic Hussainabad surroundings.
  • Built By: Nawab Nasir-ud-din Haider's era
  • Timing: Exterior can generally be viewed during the day

24. Jhansi Fort

  • Location: Jhansi
  • Famous for: Its association with Rani Lakshmibai and the Revolt of 1857.
  • Built By: Raja Bir Singh Deo
  • Timing: Generally 6:00 AM to 6:00 PM

25. Rani Mahal

  • Location: Jhansi
  • Famous for: Its connection with Rani Lakshmibai and its historic palace architecture.
  • Associated With: Rani Lakshmibai
  • Timing: Generally during daytime

26. Sankat Mochan Hanuman Temple

  • Location: Varanasi
  • Famous for: Its religious importance and association with Lord Hanuman.
  • Associated With: Goswami Tulsidas
  • Timing: Varies according to temple schedule

27. Chaukhandi Stupa

  • Location: Sarnath
  • Famous for: Its Buddhist archaeological importance and distinctive stupa structure.
  • Associated With: Ancient Buddhist tradition
  • Timing: Generally during daytime

28. Sarnath Museum

  • Location: Sarnath
  • Famous for: Ancient Buddhist sculptures and the Lion Capital of Ashoka.
  • Established By: Archaeological Survey of India
  • Timing: Generally 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM; closed on Fridays

29. Hanuman Garhi

  • Location: Ayodhya
  • Famous for: Historic Hanuman temple located on a hill-like mound reached by a flight of steps.
  • Associated With: Ayodhya's religious heritage
  • Timing: According to temple schedule

30. Kanak Bhawan

  • Location: Ayodhya
  • Famous for: Its ornate temple architecture and traditional association with Rama and Sita.
  • Associated With: Ayodhya's religious heritage
  • Timing: According to temple schedule

31. Shri Krishna Janmabhoomi Temple

  • Location: Mathura
  • Famous for: Its religious and historical association with the birthplace tradition of Lord Krishna.
  • Associated With: Krishna tradition
  • Timing: According to temple schedule

32. Dwarkadhish Temple

  • Location: Mathura
  • Famous for: Its colourful architecture and religious importance dedicated to Lord Krishna.
  • Built By: Seth Gokul Das Parikh
  • Timing: According to temple schedule

33. Banke Bihari Temple

  • Location: Vrindavan
  • Famous for: One of the most prominent Krishna temples of Vrindavan and its devotional traditions.
  • Associated With: Swami Haridas
  • Timing: According to temple schedule

34. Prem Mandir

  • Location: Vrindavan
  • Famous for: White marble architecture, decorative carvings and evening illumination.
  • Built By: Jagadguru Kripalu Parishat
  • Timing: Generally morning to evening

35. Khusro Bagh

  • Location: Prayagraj
  • Famous for: Mughal-era tombs, gardens and historical architecture.
  • Associated With: Mughal dynasty
  • Timing: Generally during daytime

36. Allahabad Fort

  • Location: Prayagraj
  • Famous for: Massive Mughal fortification at the confluence region of the Ganga and Yamuna.
  • Built By: Emperor Akbar
  • Timing: Public access is restricted in parts; timings vary

37. Anand Bhawan

  • Location: Prayagraj
  • Famous for: Its historical association with the Nehru family and India's freedom movement.
  • Associated With: Nehru family
  • Timing: Generally daytime; museum schedule applies

38. Kalinjar Fort

  • Location: Banda District
  • Famous for: Ancient hill-fort architecture, temples and long historical association with Bundelkhand.
  • Associated With: Chandela rulers and later dynasties
  • Timing: Generally during daytime

39. Dashavatara Temple

  • Location: Deogarh, Lalitpur
  • Famous for: Early Hindu temple architecture and its carved panels depicting Vishnu-related themes.
  • Built During: Gupta period
  • Timing: Generally during daytime

40. Mahaparinirvana Temple

  • Location: Kushinagar
  • Famous for: Buddhist heritage and the reclining Buddha image associated with Buddha's Mahaparinirvana.
  • Associated With: Buddhist tradition
  • Timing: Generally during daytime

41. Ramabhar Stupa

  • Location: Kushinagar
  • Famous for: Buddhist archaeological heritage and its traditional association with Buddha's cremation site.
  • Associated With: Buddhist tradition
  • Timing: Generally during daytime
