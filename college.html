Explore The Beauty Of Uttar Pradesh

Uttar Pradesh is home to many historical monuments that represent India's rich history, architecture and culture. From the Taj Mahal in Agra to the Bara Imambara in Lucknow, the state has a fascinating heritage.

Popular Distination In Uttar Pradesh

Taj Mahal

  • Location: Agra
  • Famous for: Beauty
  • Made By: Akbar
  • Opening Time: 6:00Am
  • Closing Time: 6:00Pm
  • Days available: Full moon night, plus two days before and two days after (5 nights total per month, excluding Fridays and Ramadan)
  • Timing: 8:30 PM to 12:30 AM

Agra Fort

  • Location: Agra
  • Famous for: Agra Fort is famous as a massive 16th-century UNESCO World Heritage Site that served as the main royal residence and seat of power for the Mughal Empire.
  • Made By: Akbar
  • Time: 6:00 AM to 6:00 PM (sunrise to sunset), with last entry usually permitted around 5:00 PM to 5:30 PM

Bara Imambara

  • Location: Lucknow
  • Famous for: its massive pillarless central hall, the confusing labyrinth known as Bhool Bhulaiyaa, and its origin as an 18th-century famine relief project
  • Made By: Nawab Asaf-ud-Daula
  • Time: Opening Time: 6:00 AM Closing Time: 5:00 PM

Jhansi Fort

  • Location: Jhansi
  • Famous for: the historic stronghold where Rani Lakshmibai heroically led the fight against British colonial rule during the Revolt of 1857
  • Made By: Raja Bir Singh
  • Time: open daily from 6:00 AM to 6:00 PM
